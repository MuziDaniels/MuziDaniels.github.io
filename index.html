I am a Software Engineer currently in a Learnership at Standard Bank South Africa (through March 2027).

My day to day at SB involves the migration of card processing systems to the cloud via the SmartVista Issuing Platform (SVIP), transitioning legacy transactional architectures to AWS. Additionally, I act as the Subject Matter Expert (SME) for Hermes, a microservices-based Go system consolidating transaction alerting metrics from Splunk, AppDynamics, and Grafana across OpenShift and AWS ECS clusters.
